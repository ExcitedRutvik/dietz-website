## H1: Glossary: Suspension systems explained simply
Suspension systems are mechanisms that use springs to absorb shocks and vibrations in vehicles or other mechanical systems. In vehicles, suspension systems are used to compensate for bumps in the road and improve ride comfort. These systems often include shock absorbers in combination with different types of springs. The design of effective suspension systems requires a thorough understanding of the dynamic properties of springs and the specific requirements of the application.
