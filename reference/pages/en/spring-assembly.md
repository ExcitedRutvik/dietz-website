### H1: Glossary: Spring assembly explained simply
Spring assembly refers to the process of installing or attaching springs in a mechanical system. This step is critical to ensure that the spring is properly positioned and secured. Spring assembly requires precise handling and alignment to ensure optimal spring function. In various applications, from industrial machinery to vehicles, correct spring assembly plays a key role in the performance and reliability of the overall system.
