## H1: Glossary: Spring material explained simply
The spring material refers to the material from which a spring is made. Springs can be made of different materials such as steel, stainless steel, titanium, plastic or alloys. The choice of the appropriate spring material depends on the specific requirements of the application, including the required strength, elasticity, corrosion resistance and thermal stability. Choosing the right spring material is crucial to ensure that the spring can withstand the stresses in its environment and maintain its function over a long period of time.
