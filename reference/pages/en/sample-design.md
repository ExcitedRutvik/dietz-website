### H1: Glossary: Sample design explained simply
Sample design refers to the process of designing and planning samples that serve as templates for production. In the context of products, sample designs can include textile patterns, graphic designs or even physical models. Effective sample design is critical to making the final product visually appealing, functional and meeting specific requirements. Designers often use specialised software tools and creative processes to design samples that serve as the basis for sample development and creation.
