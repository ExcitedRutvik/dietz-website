# The Ultimate Guide to Disc Spring Manufacturers

## What are disc springs?

Disc springs, also known as disc springs or Belleville springs, are a type of engineering springs that consist of concentric discs. They are characterized by their high spring force and their flat design. Disc springs are often used in applications where high axial loads are required in limited installation space. In this article we will go into more detail about choosing a disc spring manufacturer.

## How do disc springs work?

Due to their conical shape, disc springs act like coil springs. When an axial load is applied to the disc spring, the discs compress and store energy. This energy is released as the load decreases, causing the disc spring to regain its original shape.

### Applications of disc springs

Disc springs are used in a variety of applications including:

Automotive industry: Disc springs are used in clutches, shock absorbers and brake systems.

Mechanical engineering: Disc springs are used in gearboxes, clutches, valves and bearings.

Aerospace: Disc springs are used in aircraft and rockets for various applications.

Electronics industry: Disc springs are used for electrical contacting in switches and connectors.

Medical technology: Disc springs are used in medical devices for precision applications.
