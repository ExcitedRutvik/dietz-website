Tension springs are mechanical springs that are used to create traction. They consist of a wire wound in a helical shape. These springs are used to maintain tension and pressure by expanding and contracting to hold parts in place.

Threaded inserts are small metal elements that are threaded. They are inserted into materials such as plastic or wood to create a threaded connection. These threaded inserts provide a firm and reliable attachment option for tension springs.
