## H1: Glossary: Tension spring explained simply
Tension springs are springs that are used to tighten or tension components or elements. These springs are often used in combination with other mechanisms to create a mechanical preload. Tension springs are used in a variety of applications, from industrial machinery to tools and fixtures. Due to their ability to absorb and transmit tensile forces, tension springs help to ensure the stability of assemblies. When selecting tension springs, factors such as spring constant, choice of material and geometric properties are decisive.
