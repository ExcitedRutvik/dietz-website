### H1: Small compression springs: How they work
Small compression springs are tiny yet indispensable tools that play a vital role in various applications. Despite their size often being overlooked, they are essential for the functionality of many mechanical systems.

### H2: Introduction to Small Compression Springs
These are mechanical components designed to exert axial force when compressed, made from wire wound into a spiral with constant diameter, typically high-quality steel. The springs operate on potential energy principles — storing energy when compressed and releasing it when force is removed.

- Industrial Machines

### H2: Common Applications of Small Compression Springs
Locking mechanisms, electronic connections, valves, vehicle components, toys.
