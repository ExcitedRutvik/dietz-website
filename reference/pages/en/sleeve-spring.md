### H1: Glossary: Sleeve spring explained simply
A sleeve spring is a special type of spring that is wrapped in a cylindrical shape with a sleeve or casing. This design provides additional protection for the spring and allows precise control of the spring movement. Sleeve springs are often used in applications where uniform force distribution and reduced friction are required. Selecting the right material and construction for sleeve springs is critical to ensure their performance and longevity in various applications.
