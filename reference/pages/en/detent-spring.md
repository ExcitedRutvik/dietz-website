# Glossary: Detent spring explained simply

Detent springs are special springs that are used to fix components in a specific position. Due to their special shape, these springs lock in a predefined latching point and thus offer stability and security in various mechanical applications. Detent springs are often used in switch mechanisms, latches or other assemblies to ensure clear positioning. The selection of the right detent spring depends on the requirements for holding force, material properties and the design of the overall system. Due to their wide range of applications, detent springs play an important role in mechanical engineering.
