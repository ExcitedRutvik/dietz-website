### H1: Precision springs
Dietz GmbH is a renowned manufacturer of precision springs for various branches of industry. We have been experts in this field for more than 50 years, providing our customers with the highest quality and tailor-made solutions for their specific requirements. Our extensive range includes a variety of precision springs in various shapes, sizes and materials. We manufacture, among other things, compression springs, tension springs, torsion springs and torsion springs. In doing so, we can draw on state-of-the-art manufacturing technologies as well as many years of experience in the development and production of precision springs.
