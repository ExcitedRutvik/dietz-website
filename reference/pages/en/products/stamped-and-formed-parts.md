### H2: Stamped and formed parts
Dietz GmbH is a renowned company with many years of experience and expertise in the production of stamped and bent parts. We offer our customers high-quality and precisely manufactured parts for a wide range of applications and industries.

Our aim is to deliver tailor-made solutions that meet the individual requirements of our customers. To achieve this, we rely on modern manufacturing technologies and innovative processes to ensure first-class quality and efficient production.

Our stamped and bent parts are characterized by their precise shape, their high stability and their optimal accuracy of fit. We process a wide range of materials, such as metals, engineering plastics or LSR (Liquid Silicone Rubber), and can therefore offer a wide range of solutions.

#### H4: Pressure spring
Compensation of length tolerances in a seat assembly installed in the vehicle. Lowest material consumption due to a progressive composite concept without pilot strips.

#### H4: Roller Shutter Leather
Retaining spring for the axle of a parcel shelf roller blind in the trunk. Simple tool concept for small quantities and tight tolerances in the formed "bucket".

#### H4: Schuko Clamp
Use in state-of-the-art and modular socket systems for the smart home sector. Use of alloys that are cheaper than the non-ferrous metals used as standard – without sacrificing quality.

#### H4: Welding rings
Gasket insert in the engine compartment of a car. Implementation of a flexible tool for a wide range of wire and ring diameters. Mass is an essential factor for component function.

### H3: Stamped-bent parts from Dietz
Our experienced team ensures that all production steps are carefully planned and executed. From design to tooling to manufacturing, we work closely with our customers to meet their exact requirements.

Thanks to our many years of experience and extensive expertise, we are able to implement even complex requirements. Our state-of-the-art machinery and qualified employees enable us to respond efficiently and flexibly to customer requirements.

Quality is our top priority. Our stamped and bent parts go through the strictest quality controls to ensure that they meet the specified requirements. We rely on state-of-the-art measurement and testing technologies to ensure high precision and consistent quality.

Our goal is to provide our customers with not only high-quality products, but also a comprehensive service. We support our customers in the development and optimisation of their products and support them with our expertise and experience.

Dietz GmbH is proud to be a reliable partner for companies from various industries. Our stamped and bent parts are used in the automotive industry, electrical engineering, medical technology and many other areas. We are committed to exceeding our customers' expectations and building long-term partnerships.

If you are looking for high-quality and precise stamped and bent parts, you have come to the right place. We look forward to supporting you in your projects and finding successful solutions together.
