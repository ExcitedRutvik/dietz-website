### H1: Glossary: Spring force explained simply
The spring force is the force acting on a spring when it is stretched or compressed. It is directly proportional to the elongation or compression of the spring and depends on the spring constant. The spring force can be calculated by multiplying the spring constant by the deformation of the spring. In technical applications, spring force is often used to compensate for movements, transfer forces or fulfil storage functions. A precise understanding of spring force is crucial for the correct application of springs in various engineering disciplines.
