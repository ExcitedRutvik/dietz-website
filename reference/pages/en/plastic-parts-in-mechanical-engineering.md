### H2: Introduction to plastic parts production in mechanical engineering
The use of plastic parts in mechanical engineering offers a variety of advantages. Plastics are lightweight, corrosion-resistant, and can be manufactured in various shapes and sizes. In addition, they enable complex designs and offer a high degree of flexibility in design.

### H2: Advantages of using plastic parts in mechanical engineering
Plastic parts offer a number of advantages over traditional metal parts. They are lighter, which leads to a reduction in the weight of the machines and reduces energy consumption. Plastics are also resistant to corrosion, which increases the durability of the parts and reduces the need for regular maintenance.

In addition, plastics offer a high degree of design freedom. They can be formed into almost any shape and enable complex geometries that are difficult to achieve with metal parts. This allows for optimal design of parts to meet the needs of each project.

### H2: Types of plastics commonly used in mechanical engineering
1. **Polyamide (PA)** — known for high strength, rigidity and wear resistance; commonly used for gears, bearing bushings and other mechanical components.
2. **Polyethylene (PE)** — lightweight with good chemical resistance; often used for pipes, tanks and other fluid handling applications.
3. **Polycarbonate (PC)** — transparent with high impact resistance; widely used for windows, protective coverings and other applications requiring good visibility.
