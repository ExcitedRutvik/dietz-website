## H1: Glossary: Spring standards simply explained
Spring standards are standardised guidelines and specifications that regulate the manufacture, quality and use of springs. These standards are defined by international organisations such as the ISO (International Organization for Standardisation) or national standardisation bodies. They include parameters such as material properties, spring dimensions, load requirements and test methods. Compliance with spring standards is crucial to ensure the interoperability, safety and quality of springs in various industries.
