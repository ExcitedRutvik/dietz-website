# Glossary: 3D printing explained simply

3D printing, also known as additive manufacturing, is a manufacturing process in which three-dimensional objects are built up layer by layer. This is done by depositing materials such as plastics, metals or ceramics. 3D printing enables the production of complex structures and prototypes with high precision. This process is used in various industries, from the manufacture of prototypes to the production of customised components. The technology has developed rapidly in recent years and plays a crucial role in the modern manufacturing industry.
