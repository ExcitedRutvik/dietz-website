### H1: Glossary: Spring characteristics explained simply
The spring characteristics describe the specific mechanical properties of a spring that determine its reaction to external loads. This includes parameters such as spring constant, spring stiffness, spring load limit and damping. The characteristics of a spring influence its possible uses in various applications. For example, a spring with a stiff characteristic is suitable for applications that require high forces, while a softer characteristic is suitable for applications with lower loads. Precise knowledge of the spring characteristics is crucial for the selection and design of springs in technical systems.
