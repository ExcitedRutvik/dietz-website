### H1: Glossary: Sample parts explained simply
Sample parts are physical specimens or components that are produced during the sample development and production process. These parts serve as representative models for the final product and are used to verify design aspects, functionality and quality. Sample parts can be found in various industries, from the automotive industry to electronics production. Careful production and testing of sample parts is crucial to ensure that the final product meets requirements and expectations.
