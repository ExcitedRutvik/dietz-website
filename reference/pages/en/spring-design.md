### H1: Glossary: Spring design explained simply
Spring design refers to the process of developing and designing a spring according to the requirements of a specific application. This process includes selecting the appropriate spring material, defining the spring dimensions and determining the spring characteristics. Effective spring design takes into account not only the mechanical aspects, but also the environmental conditions and load requirements. The use of modern design techniques and simulations plays an important role in optimising the spring design for maximum performance and reliability.
