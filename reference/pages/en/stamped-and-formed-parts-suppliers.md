## H2: Types of stamped and bent parts

There are a variety of stamped and formed parts that can be used for various applications. Here are some of the most common types of stamped-bent parts:

gas springs: Gas springs are often used in the automotive and furniture industries to open and close flaps and hatches in a gentle and controlled manner. Compression springs with screw thread: These springs are used to build and store pressure. They are used in various applications such as valves, shock absorbers and industrial machinery. Knurled feathers: Knurled springs have a ridged surface that allows for better adhesion and grip. They find use in tools, handles and other applications where a non-slip surface is required. Other types of feathers: There are many other types of stamped parts, such as torsion springs, tension springs and torsion springs, designed for specific applications.
