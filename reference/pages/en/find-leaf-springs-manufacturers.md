## Introduction to leaf springs

Leaf springs are an important component of the chassis of vehicles. They are used to stabilize the vehicle and absorb shocks on uneven roads. Leaf springs consist of several spring blades that are connected to each other by spring bolts. They are available in different sizes and finishes to meet the different needs of different types of vehicles.
