## Heading Structure

### H1: Glossary: Spring labelling explained simply
Spring labelling involves the marking or labelling of springs to provide information about their specifications, origin or areas of application. Typical labelling may include spring constant, material type, date of manufacture or manufacturer information. Spring labelling is important for the identification and tracking of springs during their life cycle. This facilitates maintenance work, quality control and compliance with standards. Clear and precise labelling is therefore crucial to ensure the correct use and handling of springs in various applications.
