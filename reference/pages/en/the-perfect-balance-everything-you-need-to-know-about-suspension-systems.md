# The perfect balance: everything you need to know about suspension systems

## What are suspension systems?

Suspension systems are parts of a vehicle that ensure that shocks and vibrations that occur while driving are absorbed. They usually consist of various components, including springs, shock absorbers and stabilizers.

## The function of suspension systems

The main function of a suspension system is to keep the wheels of a vehicle on the road and to compensate for uneven road conditions. By absorbing shocks and vibrations, ride comfort is improved and the vehicle frame is protected from damage.

## Types of suspension systems

There are different types of suspension systems that are used in vehicles. The most common types are

### 1. independent suspension

With independent suspension, each wheel is suspended independently of the other. This enables better adaptation to uneven road surfaces and improves driving comfort.

### 2nd rigid axle

The rigid axle is a simple and robust type of suspension in which both wheels of an axle are connected to each other. It is particularly suitable for vehicles that transport heavy loads.

### 3. air suspension

The air suspension uses compressed air to spring the vehicle. It offers greater vehicle control and improved driving comfort, especially for heavy or luxury vehicles.

## Maintenance and repair of suspension systems

To ensure the optimum performance and service life of your vehicle, it is important to carry out regular maintenance work on the suspension systems. This includes regular inspections, replacing worn parts and setting the correct vehicle height.

If necessary, suspension systems should also be repaired or replaced. A specialist can help you find the best options for your vehicle.

Overall, suspension systems are of great importance for vehicle performance and ride comfort. By expanding your knowledge of these systems, you can achieve the perfect balance between comfort and safety and optimize the driving experience.
