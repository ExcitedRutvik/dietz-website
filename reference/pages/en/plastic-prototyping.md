### H1: Plastic prototyping: the innovation
In today's fast-paced world, it's crucial for companies to swiftly bring their products to market. Plastic prototyping is an innovative solution to expedite this process.

### H2: What is Plastic Prototyping?
Plastic prototyping involves creating models or prototypes of a product using plastic. These prototypes serve to test the design, identify potential flaws or improvements, and verify functionality before the product enters production.
