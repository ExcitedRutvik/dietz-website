## H2: Types of engineering springs

As a technical spring manufacturer, we offer a variety of spring types for different applications. Here are some of the most common types of engineering springs:

Compression springs: These springs are used to apply axial load and create a restoring force. They are commonly used in devices such as machinery, vehicles and electronic devices. Tension springs: Tension springs are designed to exert a pulling force and elongate when pulled apart. They are often used in applications such as garage doors, scales and locking mechanisms. Torsion springs: These springs are used to apply torque and create a restoring force. They are used in products such as doors, flaps and locks. coil springs: Coil springs are specially shaped springs that are installed into screws or bolts to create additional spring action. They are commonly used in areas such as vehicles, construction and mechanical engineering.

## H2: Manufacturing process of technical springs

The production of technical springs requires a high level of precision and expertise. As a technical spring manufacturer, we attach great importance to high-quality materials, modern production processes and strict quality controls. Here is an overview of our manufacturing process:

Material selection: We only use high quality materials such as stainless steel, carbon steel or other specific alloys depending on the requirements of each application. Design and construction: Our experienced engineers design and construct the technical springs considering customer’s specific requirements. Factors such as load, environmental factors and service life are taken into account. Material processing: The selected material is precisely machined to obtain the desired shape and size of the spring. This can include various machining processes such as cutting, bending and grinding. Heat treatment: The engineering springs undergo heat treatment to improve their strength, elasticity and durability. This step is crucial to ensure that the springs can withstand the stresses they are subjected to. Quality control: Every single step of the manufacturing process is strictly monitored and tested to ensure that the engineered springs meet the required quality standards. We use advanced testing methods such as tensile testing, compression testing and visual inspection to ensure the quality of our products.
