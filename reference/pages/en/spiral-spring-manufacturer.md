### H2: Introduction to Spiral Springs
Spiral springs are a type of spring commonly used across various industries. They are known for providing high spring force and excellent damping. Spiral springs consist of a metal spiral that compresses under pressure and returns to its original shape upon relief. There are different types of spiral springs, including tension springs, compression springs, and torsion springs.

#### H3: Various Types of Spiral Springs and Their Applications
- **Tension Springs** — used to generate tension forces; found in doors, flaps, and other mechanical devices requiring a pulling force.
- **Compression Springs** — create compressive force to hold or separate components; used in valves, switches, and punching machines.
- **Torsion Springs** — generate torsional forces; used in watches, toys, and electronic devices where a twisting force is required.

#### H3: The Manufacturing Process at a Spiral Spring Manufacturer
- **Material Selection** — stainless steel, carbon steel, or alloyed steel depending on requirements.
- **Wire Drawing** — wire drawn through multiple drawplates to reduce thickness and achieve desired strength/elasticity.
- **Spring Coiling** — wire coiled into a spiral to achieve desired shape/size, manually or by machine.
- **Heat Treatment** — enhances strength and elasticity.
- **Finishing** — cutting to length/shape, grinding, polishing, or coating as needed.
