## H1: Glossary: Spring length explained simply
The spring length refers to the free, unloaded length of a spring when it is in its natural state. This length is an important parameter in determining the spring characteristics and its possible applications. In the case of stretched or compressed springs, the actual length is changed compared to the free length, which influences the spring force. The spring length is a decisive factor in the design of spring systems, as it is directly related to the spring constant and other mechanical properties.
