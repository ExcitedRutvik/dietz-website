### H1: Glossary: Spring coiling machines explained simply
Automatic spring coiling machines are specialised machines used for the automatic production of springs. These machines enable a precise and efficient manufacturing process in which wire is automatically coiled, bent and cut to achieve the desired spring shape. Spring coiling machines play a crucial role in the mass production of springs for various applications, from industrial machinery to electronic devices. Automating this process contributes to cost efficiency and consistency in spring manufacturing.
