## H2: Types of Steel Springs and Their Applications

Steel springs come in various configurations suitable for different applications. Here are some of the most common types of steel springs and their applications:

Compression Springs: Compression springs are springs that compress and generate an opposing force when released. They are used in various applications where a restoring force is required, such as in vehicles, household appliances, and industrial machinery. Compression springs are typically cylindrical or conical in shape and come in various sizes and load classes. Tension Springs: Tension springs are springs that stretch and generate a pulling force when extended. They are commonly used in applications requiring a restoring force where space is limited, such as in electronic devices, toys, and furniture. Tension springs are available in different shapes, including cylindrical, conical, and double-wound springs. Torsion Springs: Torsion springs are springs that twist and generate a rotational force. They are used in applications requiring rotation or twisting, such as in doors, flaps, and valves. Torsion springs come in various forms, including spiral springs, flat coil springs, and leg springs.
