# The perfect solution for smooth installation: the sleeve spring

## What is a sleeve spring?

The sleeve spring is a special type of compression spring that consists of a cylindrical metal tube. It is often used in fastening technology to create a secure and reliable connection between two components. The sleeve spring works on compression and offers a high spring force with a low installation depth.

## Properties of the sleeve spring

The sleeve spring is characterized by its compact design and high spring force. It is available in various sizes and materials, including stainless steel and galvanized steel. This allows the sleeve spring to be adapted to different requirements.

## Advantages of the sleeve spring

The sleeve spring offers several advantages that make it the perfect solution for smooth installation:

Simple installation: The sleeve spring is quick and easy to fit, saving time and labor.
Secure connection: The sleeve spring ensures a secure and reliable connection between the components, guaranteeing a high level of stability.
Compact design: The sleeve spring requires very little space, which makes it particularly suitable for applications with limited installation space.
High spring force: The sleeve spring offers a high spring force with a low installation depth, making it the ideal choice for applications with high loads.
Adaptability: The sleeve spring is available in different sizes and materials to meet the specific requirements of each project.

## Application areas of the sleeve spring

The sleeve spring is used in various branches of industry, including the automotive industry, electrical engineering, the furniture industry and mechanical engineering. It is used, for example, for fastening components, damping vibrations and adjusting components.

The sleeve spring offers the ideal solution for companies that require smooth assembly and a secure connection between components.
