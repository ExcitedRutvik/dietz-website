### H1: Glossary: Prototyping explained simply
Prototyping is the process of producing physical models or prototypes based on sample designs and developments. This step makes it possible to test the sample in reality, check its functionality and make any necessary adjustments before mass production. Sample creation is particularly important in areas such as the textile industry, where garments are tested before production to optimise fit, material selection and design. An efficient sample creation process contributes to quality and efficiency.
