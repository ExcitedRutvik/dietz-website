### H2: Introduction to stamped and bent parts in small series
Stamped and bent parts are an important component of many products. These parts are manufactured by punching and bending sheets of metal to achieve the desired shape and function. They are used in various industries such as the automotive industry, mechanical engineering, the electronics industry and many others.

The production of stamped and bent parts in small series requires specialized knowledge and skills. It is important that parts are precise and of high quality to ensure optimal performance. Therefore, it is important to work with an experienced supplier who has the expertise and resources to meet your needs.

### H2: Advantages of stamped and bent parts in small series
1. **Flexibility** — production in small series allows a high level of flexibility in adapting production to specific requirements; quick response to changes without high mass-production costs.
2. **Cost-effectiveness** — no need for large amounts of raw materials/inventory, reducing capital commitment and financial risk.
3. **Faster time to market** — allows products to reach market more quickly, reacting to trends and customer requirements.

### H2: Challenges in production – stamped and bent parts in small series
1. **High set-up costs** — each series requires specific tools/settings, increasing overall production cost.
2. **Supply chain bottlenecks** — sourcing materials/components in small quantities can be difficult.
3. **Quality control** — monitoring quality across batches with differing tools/settings is challenging.

### H2: Understanding of the punching and bending process
1. **Material selection** — choosing suitable materials based on strength, durability, corrosion resistance requirements.
2. **Punching process** — a die cuts the desired pattern/shape from sheet metal at high speed.
3. **Bending process** — punched parts are bent into shape using special tools/dies in a bending machine.
4. **Post-processing** — deburring, grinding, or surface treatment to achieve desired surface quality.

### H2: Tools and equipment for the production of stamped and bent parts
1. **Punching tools** — punch and die working together to cut shapes.
2. **Bending machines** — press brakes, roller benders, etc., to bend parts.
3. **Cutting tools** — scissors or electric clippers to size sheet metal before punching.
4. **Measuring tools** — calipers, micrometers, optical measuring systems for quality checks.

### H2: Quality control measures for small series production
1. **Sampling** — checking a representative selection of parts (visual inspection, measurement, functional tests).
2. **Process monitoring** — monitoring machine parameters, tools, production times.
3. **Documentation and traceability** — documenting all steps/results to identify problems and improve.

### H2: Cost considerations for the production of small series
1. **Material costs** — choosing materials meeting requirements and budget.
2. **Labor costs** — wages, benefits, other employment costs.
3. **Setup costs** — preparing machines/tools/equipment for each series (can be high for small series).
4. **Quality control costs** — inspections, measurements, testing.
