## H2: Eigenschaften von Flachdraht-Druckfedern
1. High load capacity and excellent spring force
2. Compact design for space-limited applications
3. High stability with repeated loading
4. Long service life through robust construction

## H2: Vorteile von Flachdraht-Druckfedern
1. Höhere Federkraft im Vergleich zu herkömmlichen runden Drahtfedern
2. Space-saving flat profile design
3. Uniform force distribution across spring length
4. Reduced friction improving longevity and efficiency

## H2: Anwendungen von Flachdraht-Druckfedern
Applications across four industries: automotive (suspension, brakes, seats), mechanical engineering (valve control, tool suspension), electrical (switches, connectors), medical technology (implants, syringes).

2. Wire bending via specialized machines
3. Heat treatment for strength/elasticity
4. End processing (grinding, cutting, eye/hook attachment)
