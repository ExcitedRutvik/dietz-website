## H1: Alles über Druckfedern nach DIN 2095

Druckfedern sind eine essenzielle Komponente in vielen technischen Anwendungen. Sie werden verwendet, um Kräfte auszugleichen, Kompression zu erzeugen und mechanische Bewegungen zu unterstützen. In diesem Artikel erfahren Sie alles, was Sie über Druckfedern nach DIN 2095 wissen müssen.

### H2: Was sind Druckfedern nach DIN 2095?

Druckfedern belong to the screw spring category and compress under pressure. They consist of constant-diameter wire wound spirally. When force is applied, they compress and store energy; upon force removal, they expand and release stored energy.

### H2: Die Bedeutung von DIN 2095

DIN 2095 is a German standard defining requirements for compression springs, including geometric dimensions, materials, tolerances, and load behavior. Springs manufactured per this standard meet industrial requirements.

### H2: Merkmale von Druckfedern nach DIN 2095

#### H3: Drahtdurchmesser (d)

The wire diameter, measured in millimeters, is crucial for spring strength and load capacity. Thicker wire produces stronger springs.

#### H3: Außendurchmesser (D)

Maximum spring diameter, measured in millimeters, varies by application and load requirements. Larger diameters typically indicate higher load capacity.

#### H3: Freie Länge (L0)

This represents the spring's total length under no pressure, measured in millimeters. It's important for proper spring selection.

#### H3: Windungen (n)

The number of spirals formed by the wire. More windings increase flexibility and compressibility.

#### H3: Federkennlinie

This describes the relationship between applied force and resulting deformation. Types include linear, progressive, or degressive, depending on application requirements.

### H2: Auswahl der richtigen Druckfedern nach DIN 2095

#### H3: Belastung

Determine maximum load the spring must withstand, considering both static and potential dynamic loads.

#### H3: Umgebung

Account for environmental conditions like temperature, humidity, corrosion, and vibration affecting spring performance.

#### H3: Federkonstante

This measures spring stiffness, indicating force needed to compress or extend the spring by a specific amount.

#### H3: Material

Spring material affects strength, durability, and corrosion resistance. Common materials include steel alloys like stainless or spring steel.

#### H3: Toleranzen

Check DIN 2095 tolerances to ensure they match application requirements, verifying acceptable deviations from specifications.

### H2: Fazit: Druckfedern nach DIN 2095

Compression springs per DIN 2095 provide necessary support and compression for balancing mechanical movements and distributing forces. With proper information and selection, you can find manufacturers meeting your requirements for quality springs. Consider specific application needs and select appropriate parameters.
