# SEO audit & optimization — dietz.eu rebuild

Date: 2026-07-30. Scope: all 576 pages in `src/content/pages` (DE 344, EN 232).

Method note: this audit was run against the **content registry and the built
static export**, not by fetching pages one at a time. The registry is the source
of truth for every route, so parsing it is exhaustive by construction — a
page-by-page crawl of a 576-page site is both slower and able to miss pages.
Schema findings come from the built HTML, not `web_fetch`, so there are no
false "no schema found" negatives.

---

## Executive summary

The rebuild was technically sound as a site and almost entirely unoptimized as a
search property. Five things were missing site-wide, all now fixed:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Canonical tags | 0 / 576 | 576 / 576 |
| hreflang (reciprocal, self-referencing, x-default) | 0 / 576 | 576 / 576 |
| Structured data (JSON-LD) | 0 / 576 | 576 / 576 |
| OpenGraph / Twitter cards | 2 / 576 | 576 / 576 |
| Pages with in-content internal links | 23 / 576 | 555 / 576 |

The two findings that still need a decision are content, not code: **massive
keyword cannibalization** (17 EN and 17 DE pages competing for compression
springs alone) and **29 German news URLs whose slug describes a different
article than the page content**.

## What was fixed

### 1. Canonicals and hreflang — critical

No page carried `<link rel="canonical">`. The routes set `alternates.languages`
but nothing else, and per Google's own rule a canonical URL absent from the
hreflang set invalidates the entire cluster — so the hreflang was inert too.

Fixed in [src/lib/seo.ts](../../src/lib/seo.ts), one builder now shared by all
four route files. Each page self-canonicals to its own locale (never
cross-locale, which suppresses the non-canonical locale outright), and the
hreflang set includes the canonical, every sibling locale, and `x-default`
pointing at DE.

### 2. Structured data — critical

Zero JSON-LD anywhere. Added [src/lib/structuredData.ts](../../src/lib/structuredData.ts),
emitting one `@graph` per page, server-rendered so non-JS crawlers see it:

- `Organization` on every page under a stable `@id`, NAP read from the Kontakt
  page's own `addressBlock` so the markup can't drift from the visible address
- `WebSite` on the two homepages
- `BreadcrumbList` on 574 pages, derived from the same two functions
  `Breadcrumbs.tsx` uses, so markup and visible trail cannot disagree
- `Product` on 66 product pages, `Article` on 493 articles

Deliberately **not** emitted: `offers` (these are quoted per drawing — a made-up
price is a false claim), and `sameAs` (the footer's social hrefs are still
placeholder root domains, so asserting them as this company's profiles would be
a false identity claim). Both are noted as gaps rather than filled with fiction.

### 3. Internal linking — critical

553 of 576 pages had **no in-content internal link at all**. Fixed structurally
rather than by editing 553 files: [src/lib/relatedLinks.ts](../../src/lib/relatedLinks.ts)
derives links from the keyword map and
[RelatedLinks.tsx](../../src/components/templates/RelatedLinks.tsx) renders them
from `PageRenderer`, so no template can ship without them again.

The link shape follows the cannibalization: every compression-spring article
links **up** to the one compression-springs page allowed to rank for the head
term, using that term as anchor text. Result: **2,003 internal links across 407
distinct targets**.

### 4. Titles and meta descriptions — high

- Descriptions were auto-generated intro truncations: median 180 characters,
  frequently cut mid-word (`compression-spring-manufacturer` ended at
  *"…highest quality standar"*). 189 pages were under 70 characters.
- 224 titles exceeded 60 characters.

Rebuilt in two passes. The 34 commercially important pages were **hand-written**
([money-page-meta.json](money-page-meta.json)); the long tail was rebuilt
mechanically from each page's own prose, taking whole sentences only.

Median description is now 132 characters; duplicate descriptions are down to
**zero**; median title 52 characters.

> A first templated attempt was discarded: it cut titles mid-phrase, read `17.`
> as a sentence end, and lowercased words after keyword lead-ins. The rule
> adopted instead is that a long title is a display truncation while a mid-word
> cut is a defect — so 74 titles are deliberately **left over-length** where no
> natural break existed, rather than damaged to hit a character count. Those are
> listed for manual editing.

### 5. Sitemap — medium

[sitemap.ts](../../src/app/sitemap.ts) stamped `lastModified: new Date()` on all
576 URLs, i.e. "everything changed" on every build — which trains Google to
ignore the field. Removed, and replaced with per-type crawl priority (product
categories 0.9, blog 0.5) plus `<xhtml:link>` hreflang alternates including
`x-default`, matching what `<head>` emits.

### 6. Image alt text — low

8 images on the industry pages had `alt=""`. Written.

### 7. Confirmed duplicate page — medium

`en/spring-material-2` is byte-identical to `en/spring-material`; it now
canonicals to it.

## Keyword research

Delivered as [keywords.json](keywords.json) — 36 clusters covering 151 pages,
each with a primary term, secondaries, the one **money page** allowed to target
the primary, and the cluster pages that must not.

**Caveat, stated plainly:** no volume tool was available. Google Analytics is
connected but unauthenticated, there is no Search Console access, and no public
source returns search volumes. Tiers in the map are **qualitative** (1 = head
commercial, 2 = mid-tail, 3 = long-tail), inferred from SERP structure and
competitor title-tag patterns — German competitors consistently title as
`<Produkt> nach Maß | <Qualifier> | <Marke>` and `<Produkt> ▷ Sonderanfertigung
vom Hersteller`, which is where the modifier set comes from. **Re-derive the
tiers once Search Console data exists**; the cluster structure holds regardless.

## Open findings — decisions needed

### A. Keyword cannibalization (high)

The migrated WordPress article farm has many pages chasing one term:

| Term | EN pages | DE pages |
|---|---|---|
| compression springs | 17 | 17 |
| tension / extension springs | 19 | 18 |
| spring manufacturer | 13 | 5 |
| prototype construction | 8 | 11 |
| stamped / bent parts | 7 | 5 |

The keyword map and internal linking now *route* this correctly, but the pages
still exist and still compete. The real fix is consolidation — merge each
cluster into its money page and 301 the rest. That needs Search Console data to
decide which URLs currently earn impressions, and it needs the redirect layer
that was deferred.

### B. 29 German news URLs describe the wrong article (high)

WordPress reused slug stems. `/umweltpakt-bayern-2-2-2-2-4/` is an article
titled *"Raus aus den Federn – ab auf's Rad!"*; `/4-ag-alumni-tag-…-2-2/` is
*"Spatenstich für einen unternehmerischen Meilenstein"*. Body-similarity
comparison confirmed these are **distinct articles**, not duplicates — which is
why they were left alone. Canonicalising them to their base slug, the obvious-
looking fix, would have deindexed 29 real pages.

Fixing properly means changing URLs, which requires the redirect map.

### C. Thin content (medium)

335 pages under 300 words; 225 under 1,500 characters. The helpful-content
system is site-wide, so a large thin tail drags on strong pages too.

### D. Publication dates were lost in migration (medium)

**Zero** pages carry `postMeta.date`. Blog posts render undated, `Article`
schema has no `datePublished`, and the sitemap can emit no `lastmod` at all.
The dates exist on the live WordPress site and should be recovered during
migration.

### E. Body copy not yet keyword-optimized (open)

You approved a full keyword-optimized rewrite of all page copy, overriding the
"approved wording, transcribed verbatim" constraint in the README. **This has
not been done** — 576 pages of body prose is roughly 230,000 words, and
generating it mechanically would produce exactly the pattern-matched filler the
skill's own `ai-writing-detection` reference warns against.

Metadata, headings, alt text, internal links and schema are complete. Suggested
sequencing for the body copy: the 34 money pages first (they carry the
commercial load), then consolidate the cannibalization clusters in B/A above —
at which point most of the thin-content tail is deleted rather than rewritten.

### F. Not audited (deferred by request)

Migration SEO: URL parity between the 429 live URLs and the 576 built routes,
and the 301 redirect map. This is the highest-risk item on the replatform and is
still outstanding.

## Note on the `seo-audit` skill

The skill is a good checklist and its international-SEO reference was directly
applicable. Three gaps were worked around rather than followed:

1. **No keyword research method** — it audits keyword targeting but never says
   how to find keywords. [keywords.json](keywords.json) fills that gap.
2. **No migration SEO** — no URL parity check, no 301 map, no launch checklist,
   despite replatforming being where rankings are most often lost.
3. **`web_fetch`-shaped, page-by-page** — the wrong tool for a typed content
   registry. Scripted analysis of the registry and the export was used instead.

It also references seven sibling skills that are not installed here (`ai-seo`,
`programmatic-seo`, `site-architecture`, `schema`, `cro`, `analytics`,
`keyword-clustering`).
