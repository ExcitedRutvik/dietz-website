# Backlink opportunities — Dietz GmbH

**Every prospect below is evidenced by something Dietz already publishes.** These
are not generic outreach targets; each one is an organisation Dietz demonstrably
belongs to, is certified by, or partners with, where a member or holder listing
either already exists or can be requested as a matter of fact rather than of
persuasion. That is what makes them realistic: the relationship exists, only the
link is missing.

No prospect here is invented, and there are no volume or difficulty estimates —
we have no Ahrefs or Semrush data for this domain, and a fabricated metric is
worse than none. Getting Search Console access is the first item in "What we
need from you" for exactly this reason.

## Tier 3 — industry directories

Lower value individually, and worth doing once and properly rather than at scale.

- **Wer liefert was (wlw)** and **Kompass** — the two directories German
  procurement actually uses.
- **IndustryArena**, **Techpilot** — relevant to a contract manufacturer.
- Avoid paid link packages and general business directories. For a supplier
  whose buyers are engineers, they add nothing and carry real risk.

## What would make this measurable

We cannot tell you which of these Dietz already has, or what any of them is
worth, without:

1. **Google Search Console access** — shows existing referring domains, which
   queries already earn impressions, and which pages compete with each other.
2. **Google Analytics access** — shows which referrers actually send people who
   enquire.

With those two, this list becomes a ranked plan instead of a well-evidenced
starting point. Without them, any number we attached would be invented.

## One thing to fix before link-building

Link-building into a page that competes with its own sibling wastes the link.
Two issues to settle first:

- **17 German and 17 English pages target compression-spring terms.** Search
  Console data is needed to decide which to keep, which to merge, and which to
  redirect. Building links before that decision means pointing them at pages
  that may be consolidated away.
- **`sameAs` is deliberately absent from the site's Organization markup**
  because the footer's social links point at platform root domains rather than
  at Dietz profiles. Real profile URLs for LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram and
  Facebook would let us complete the entity markup — which is how search engines
  and AI assistants confirm that all these mentions refer to one company.
